### [0:45 – 1:45] Slide 2: Problem Statement & Research Objectives
**Slide Displayed**: Slide 2 (Split-Screen: Agricultural Vulnerabilities & Technological Imperative)

**Speaker Action**: Point towards the field reality image on the right; speak with passion about smallholder vulnerabilities.

> "Distinguished scientists, Telangana's 6 million farming families are facing a critical triad of operational vulnerabilities:
>
> 1. **Acute Rural Labor Deficits**: Peak transplantation and weeding windows now face unprecedented labor shortages, inflating manual costs by ₹5,000 to ₹8,000 per acre.
> 2. **Midnight Irrigation Hazards**: Because 3-phase agricultural electricity is predominantly supplied during erratic off-peak night hours, smallholder farmers must venture into pitch-dark fields at 1:00 AM, routinely resulting in fatal electrocutions and snakebites, alongside massive aquifer depletion.
> 3. **The Chemical Overdose and 'DAP Nitrogen Trap'**: Smallholder farmers rely on commercial pesticide dealers who prescribe unscientific chemical mixtures. Furthermore, farmers routinely apply Di-Ammonium Phosphate (DAP) solely for phosphorus, oblivious to the fact that a 50 kg bag delivers 9.4 kg of elemental Nitrogen. They apply full urea doses on top, inducing vegetative toxicity, severe lodging, and explosive Rice Blast epidemics.
>
> **Our Research Objective**: *To design, build, and deploy an integrated, bilingual AI and IoT digital platform that bridges 3,689 pages of verified PJTSAU research with ESP32 edge telemetry — establishing closed-loop precision irrigation, CIBRC-compliant crop safety, and verifiable farmer decision support towards unmanned farming by 2047.*"

**Slide Displayed**: Slide 6 (IoT Closed-Loop Workflow) then Slide 7 (TACH-10 Benchmark & Economic Impact)

**Speaker Action**: Emphasize empirical data and field measurements.

> "On Slide 6, our IoT edge architecture interfaces an **ESP32 dual-core microcontroller** with corrosion-resistant capacitive soil moisture sensors and DHT22 atmospheric sensors. Telemetry streams via REST/WebSocket into Firebase Realtime Database.
>
> The automated control algorithm implements hysteresis switching: when root-zone moisture drops below 30%, the 30A optocoupled pump relay energizes; once field capacity reaches 55%, it automatically cuts off — reducing agricultural water consumption by **25% to 30%**.

Evaluated on the **TACH-10 benchmark** (Telangana Agricultural Case Harness), the system achieved **100.0% Top-1 diagnostic accuracy** on evaluable crop health cases and **100.0% appropriate abstention** on blurry foliage or unsupported crops — completely eliminating false-positive diagnoses."

### Step 1: Homepage Command Center [00:00 – 00:25]
- **Action**: Navigate to `https://smartfarming-web.vercel.app/`.
- **Narration**:
  > "Judges, this is our live production interface. Notice the clean, responsive layout designed for rural smartphones. At the top, we have live weather telemetry from Jagtial district, real-time soil moisture gauges, and quick-action cards for all ten integrated modules."

### Step 2: AI Krishi Sahayak Grounded Bilingual Advisory [00:25 – 00:50]
- **Action**: Click on **AI Krishi Sahayak** (or navigate to `/advisory`).
- **Prompt Typed / Spoken**: *"Explain about trichoderma and DSR drum seeder in paddy."*
- **Visual Highlight**: Show the generated response.
- **Narration**:
  > "Observe the response. The system does not give generic advice. It retrieves the exact recommended protocol from PJTSAU publications: soaking seeds for 24 hours, incubating for 24 hours to obtain 1–2 mm radicle sprouts, using a seed rate of 10–12 kg/acre, and saving ~₹5,000/acre over manual transplanting.
  >
  > Most importantly, look at the bottom: **'Source Citation: PJTAU Vyavasayam Monthly Magazine (Page 8)'**. The knowledge is 100% verified and auditable."

### Step 3: Crop Disease Diagnosis Studio & CIBRC Safety Gate [00:50 – 01:15]
- **Action**: Click on **Diagnosis** tab (`/diagnosis`).
- **Action A (Standard Diagnosis)**: Select or upload Paddy Blast sample. Show differential diagnostic breakdown (Confidence: 94%, Causal agent: *Magnaporthe oryzae*).
- **Action B (Safety & Abstention)**: Show the CIBRC safety warning:
  > *"Recommended spray: Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L. Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 21 days. Do not spray within 21 days of harvest."*
- **Action C (Abstention Test)**: Upload a blurry image or non-plant image. Show system abstaining: *"Image quality insufficient or non-plant subject. Please upload a clear leaf photograph."*
- **Narration**:
  > "Our vision pipeline includes statutory guardrails. It calculates Pre-Harvest Intervals to prevent export rejections and strictly refuses to guess when presented with blurry photographs, safeguarding farmer investment."

### Step 4: IoT Soil Telemetry & Pump Controller [01:15 – 01:40]
- **Action**: Click on **IoT Pump** tab (`/iot`).
- **Visual Highlight**: Point to the live moisture gauge reading (e.g., 28.4%), pump status indicator, and automatic mode toggle.
- **Action**: Click to toggle manual pump override. The system immediately prompts for the **Owner Security PIN**.
- **Action**: Enter PIN `SmartFarmer@2026`.
- **Narration**:
  > "Here is our live edge telemetry stream connecting to Firebase RTDB. The current root-zone moisture reads 28.4%. Because it is below our 30% agronomic threshold, the pump status is triggered ON in automatic closed-loop mode.
  >
  > If a user attempts a manual override, our security protocol requires the farm owner PIN to prevent remote tampering or neighbor water theft."

### Step 5: Fertilizer Calculator — Solving the DAP Nitrogen Trap [01:40 – 02:00]
- **Action**: Click **All Features ➔ Agricultural Calculators** (`/tools`). Select **Fertilizer NPK Solver**.
- **Input**: Crop: Paddy | Target: Standard 1 Acre recommendation (100 kg N, 60 kg P₂O₅, 40 kg K₂O). Select Basal application using DAP.
- **Output Displayed**: Shows required DAP = 130 kg, which supplies 24 kg N; automatically deducts 24 kg N from total Urea requirement, saving ~52 kg of Urea.
- **Narration**:
  > "Finally, our deterministic nutrient calculator. When a farmer inputs DAP for phosphorus, our algorithm automatically calculates the inherent Nitrogen contribution and deducts it from the top-dress urea schedule. In one single acre of paddy, this saves the farmer 1 to 1.2 bags of urea, preventing lodging, reducing blast susceptibility, and putting ₹350 back in the farmer's pocket."
