End-to-End Workflow

How Smart Farm Assistant Works

From soil sensor probes to cloud automation, every step is built for maximum reliability, safety, and farmer simplicity.

1

Probe Reads Moisture

A soil moisture sensor probe inserted in the crop root zone measures volumetric water content continuously.

2

ESP32 Sends Telemetry

The ESP32 microcontroller averages ADC readings and transmits encrypted telemetry to Firebase Realtime Database over WiFi.

3

Real-Time Mobile Dashboard

The farmer opens the web app on any smartphone to view live soil moisture percentage and history charts.

4

Smart Automated Pumping

The farmer triggers the pump manually or Auto-Mode irrigates automatically, pausing if rain is forecast.

Responsible Technology & Advisory Guidelines

⚠️ AI Disease Diagnosis

AI crop diagnosis is an assistive screening tool. Symptoms can mimic nutrient deficiencies or soil disorders. Always confirm recommendations with your Mandal Agricultural Extension Officer (AEO) before spraying chemicals.

🧪 Fertilizer Formulations

Dosages represent university-standard baseline recommendations from the PJTAU Package of Practices (Vyavasaya Panchangam). Farmers should cross-verify values against their specific field Soil Health Card test report.

📊 Mandi Market Data

Mandi prices display official daily Agmarknet transactions for reporting Telangana APMCs. During off-season or non-reporting market days, standard reference benchmarks are provided for comparative analysis.
